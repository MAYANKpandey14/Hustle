However, what most did not understand was the sudden switch of American diplomacy and stance towards Indian escalation against Pakistan. One day before ceasefire VP J.D. The *quintessential* question is What caused this sudden change in American stance ?

***The Answer***
Indian strikes on **Nur Khan**, **Sargodha**, and **Jacobabad** air bases. All three had a substantial US presence, specifically **Technical Security Teams (TSTs)** embedded for 24/7 monitoring of F-16 assets and advanced munitions. 

Pakistan effectively used these US personnel as a "human shield" or "tripwire." When Indian precision strikes (Operation Sindoor) hit these bases to neutralize Pakistan's nuclear delivery capability, the risk of American casualties forced Washington's hand. This is the core of the "blackmail"—Pakistan forces US intervention by co-locating its strategic assets with American oversight teams.

Another twist in the story is about Pakistan nuclear arsenal and capability, which was functionally degraded/destroyed after the Indian strikes, leading to a "Use it or Lose it" panic that the US had to manage.
