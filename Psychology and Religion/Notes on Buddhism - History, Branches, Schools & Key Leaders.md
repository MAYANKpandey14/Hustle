## **1. What is Buddhism?**

**Buddhism** (Buddhadharma) is an Indian religion and philosophy based on teachings attributed to **Gautama Buddha** (Siddhartha Gautama), who lived in **6th–5th century BCE** in eastern India. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism)

- **4th largest religion** globally: ~**320 million followers** (4.1% of world population) [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism)
- Founded as a *śramaṇa* movement in the **5th century BCE**
- Spread throughout Asia and to the West in the 20th century [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism)

### **Core Goal**
Achieve **nirvana** (liberation from suffering and the cycle of rebirth/saṃsāra) through the **Middle Way** between extreme asceticism and sensual indulgence. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism)

## **2. The Buddha: Founder**

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Name** | Siddhartha Gautama (later "Buddha" = "The Awakened One")  [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism) |
| **Birth** | **Lumbini** (present-day Nepal), c. 563–483 BCE  [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism) |
| **Family** | Father: Śuddhodana (Shakya clan leader); Mother: Queen Maya  [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism) |
| **Enlightenment** | Attained **Bodhi** under the **Bodhi Tree** at **Bodh Gaya**, India  [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism) |
| **Teaching Career** | ~45 years of teaching across northern India (Bihar, Uttar Pradesh)  [malcolmtattersall.com](https://www.malcolmtattersall.com.au/big-questions/buddhist-schools/) |
| **Death** | **Parinirvana** at age 80 in **Kushinagar**, India  [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism) |

The Buddha taught the **Four Noble Truths** and the **Noble Eightfold Path** as the path to end suffering. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism)

## **3. Core Teachings of Buddhism**

### **A. The Four Noble Truths** [namchak](https://www.namchak.org/community/blog/three-different-paths-theravada-mahayana-and-vajrayana/)

| Truth | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **1. Dukkha** | Life involves suffering/unsatisfactoriness |
| **2. Samudaya** | Suffering is caused by craving/attachment (taṇhā) |
| **3. Nirodha** | Suffering can end by letting go of craving |
| **4. Marga** | The path to end suffering is the Noble Eightfold Path |

### **B. Noble Eightfold Path** [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism)

Grouped into **Three Trainings**:

| Division | Factors |
|----------|---------|
| **Wisdom (Prajñā)** | 1. Right View -  2. Right Intention |
| **Ethics (Śīla)** | 3. Right Speech -  4. Right Action -  5. Right Livelihood |
| **Meditation (Samādhi)** | 6. Right Effort -  7. Right Mindfulness -  8. Right Concentration |

### **C. Three Marks of Existence** [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism)

1. **Anicca** (Impermanence)
2. **Dukkha** (Suffering/Unsatisfactoriness)
3. **Anattā** (Non-self – no permanent soul)

### **D. Key Concepts**

| Concept | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Saṃsāra** | Cycle of rebirth/death  [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism) |
| **Karma** | Action/intent determines future rebirths  [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism) |
| **Dependent Origination** | All phenomena arise due to conditions (Twelve Nidānas)  [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism) |
| **Three Jewels** | Take refuge in **Buddha** -  **Dharma** (teachings) -  **Sangha** (monastic community)  [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism) |

## **4. Main Branches of Buddhism**

Buddhism is divided into **three major branches** (vehicles/yanas):

| Branch | Meaning | Where Practiced | Followers | Key Focus |
|--------|---------|-----------------|-----------|-----------|
| **Theravāda** | "School of Elders" | Sri Lanka, Thailand, Myanmar, Laos, Cambodia  [education.asianart](https://education.asianart.org/resources/what-are-the-main-branches-of-buddhism/) | ~170 million  [malcolmtattersall.com](https://www.malcolmtattersall.com.au/big-questions/buddhist-schools/) | Oldest form; Pali Canon; monastic path; self-liberation  [education.asianart](https://education.asianart.org/resources/what-are-the-main-branches-of-buddhism/) |
| **Mahāyāna** | "Great Vehicle" | China, Japan, Korea, Vietnam, Taiwan  [education.asianart](https://education.asianart.org/resources/what-are-the-main-branches-of-buddhism/) | ~300 million  [malcolmtattersall.com](https://www.malcolmtattersall.com.au/big-questions/buddhist-schools/) | Universal salvation; bodhisattvas help all beings  [education.asianart](https://education.asianart.org/resources/what-are-the-main-branches-of-buddhism/) |
| **Vajrayāna** | "Diamond Vehicle" | Tibet, Bhutan, Mongolia, Himalayas  [education.asianart](https://education.asianart.org/resources/what-are-the-main-branches-of-buddhism/) | ~30 million  [malcolmtattersall.com](https://www.malcolmtattersall.com.au/big-questions/buddhist-schools/) | Tantric/esoteric practices; shortcut to enlightenment  [education.asianart](https://education.asianart.org/resources/what-are-the-main-branches-of-buddhism/) |

**Note:** Vajrayāna is technically a **subset of Mahāyāna**. [tricycle](https://tricycle.org/what-is-buddhism/)

## **8. Key Differences Between Branches**

| Aspect | Theravāda | Mahāyāna | Vajrayāna |
|--------|-----------|----------|-----------|
| **Goal** | Become **Arhat** (self-liberation) | Become **Bodhisattva** (help all beings) | Become Buddha quickly (one lifetime) |
| **Buddha View** | Historical human teacher | Transcendental being; many Buddhas | Buddha-nature in all beings |
| **Texts** | Pali Canon (Tipiṭaka) | Mahāyāna Sūtras + Pali | Tantras + Mahāyāna Sūtras |
| **Practice** | Meditation + monasticism | Bodhicitta + compassion + devotion | Mantras + mandalas + deity yoga |
| **Motivation** | Free **oneself** from suffering | Free **all beings** from suffering | Free **all beings** via tantric methods |
