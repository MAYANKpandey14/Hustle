# Sales Engagement Checklist

Use this checklist to prepare for and review your sales engagements, utilizing key psychological and conversational triggers.

## Pre-Engagement Prep
- [ ] **Define Value-First Approach:** Identify one piece of insight, a useful audit, or a practical suggestion to offer during the call to trigger reciprocity.
- [ ] **Gather Social Proof:** Select 1-2 case studies or customer stories specifically relevant to the prospect's industry or current role.
- [ ] **Map the "Small Wins":** Determine a series of small, low-friction next steps you can propose instead of asking for a big commitment immediately.
- [ ] **Verify Constraints:** If you plan to use scarcity (e.g., implementation slots, pricing deadlines), ensure the limit is real and verifiable.

## During the Conversation
- [ ] **Set the Stage for Trust:** Open by framing the conversation as a two-way fit discussion, explicitly mentioning that the product might *not* be the right answer.
- [ ] **Ask Reflective Questions:** Avoid "interrogation" checklists. Use open-ended questions that force reflection (e.g., "How does your team currently handle X, and where does that process break down?").
- [ ] **Demonstrate Authority:** Teach something new or share a pattern recognition derived from real customer situations to reduce doubt.
- [ ] **Agitate Problems with Context:** Help the prospect connect their current pain to tangible consequences (lost time, revenue, risk) without using exaggerated fear.
- [ ] **Simplify the Offer:** Ensure your pitch uses plain language. Can you summarize the value in three clear reasons? Can you explain the implementation path in a simple sequence?
- [ ] **Address Risk Directly:** Ask yourself: "What is the prospect afraid will go wrong?" Proactively discuss onboarding support, realistic expectations, or phased rollouts.
- [ ] **Build Human Connection:** Listen for their priorities and language style. Acknowledge trade-offs honestly to build rapport.

## Post-Engagement Review (Self-Audit)
- [ ] **Did I lead with relevance,** or did I dump product features too early?
- [ ] **Did the prospect do most of the talking?** (Reflective vs. Yes/No answers)
- [ ] **Did I maintain trust?** Was I honest enough that the prospect felt safe sharing their true thoughts?
