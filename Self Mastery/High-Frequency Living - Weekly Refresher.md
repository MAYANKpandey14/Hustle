# High-Frequency Living: Weekly Refresher

> **The Core Shift:** Stop waiting for external success to feel peace. Cultivate the state first; the reality follows.

## 1. The Golden Rule: Non-Attachment
* **"Do not need what you want."** Needing creates a "frequency of lack" that leads to desperation and poor performance.
* **Practice:** Pursue goals with intensity, but decouple your emotional well-being from the immediate outcome.

## 2. Rebuilding Self-Trust (The Engine)
Confidence is simply the reputation you have with yourself.
* **The Fix:** Make 3 small, non-negotiable promises daily (e.g., wake time, one workout, one healthy meal).
* **The Rule:** Never break a promise to yourself. If you can't keep it, don't voice it.

## 3. Extreme Ownership & Adversity
* **Neutrality:** Events are neutral. You assign the weight.
* **The Test:** When things go wrong, do you collapse or remain in a state of "willingness"?
* **Ownership:** Blame drains power. Responsibility returns it. Take ownership of your *response* to everything.

## 4. The Abundance Loop
* **Give to Receive:** If you want more money, love, or joy, become a source of it first.
* **Action:** Tip well, offer genuine encouragement, or provide value without asking for anything in return.

---

## Weekly Checklist
- [ ] **State Check:** Am I acting from "Need" or "Abundance"?
- [ ] **Promise Audit:** Did I keep my 3 daily commitments this week?
- [ ] **Reframe:** What "bad" event this week can I re-label as "neutral/instructive"?
- [ ] **Generosity:** Where did I act as a source of value today?

## Key Mantras
* *Reality is a mirror of what you have become.*
* *You can only control your mind.*
* *You want joy? Become a source of joy.*
