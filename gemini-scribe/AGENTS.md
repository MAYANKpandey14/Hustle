# AGENTS.md

This file provides context about this Obsidian vault for AI agents.

## Vault Overview

This vault functions as a comprehensive, high-stakes knowledge base and strategic research repository for Mayank Pandey. It is primarily designed to synthesize complex real-time geopolitical intelligence—specifically regarding 2026 regional conflicts, Indian defense strategy, and global supply chains—while balancing these macro-level insights with actionable frameworks for sales psychology and personal mastery.

## Organization

The vault is organized into six thematic folders:

- **GeoPolitics & Def/**: The primary area for strategic analyses, research, and policy papers regarding Indian defense, naval presence, and regional security.
- **Business/**: Contains competitor analyses, tech stack breakdowns, and business strategy/product research notes.
- **Sales Resources/**: Focuses on the intersection of psychology, commerce, and sales enablement checklists.
- **Self Mastery/**: Dedicated to personal growth, daily productivity guides, and mindset optimization.
- **Psychology and Religion/**: Contains historical and philosophical notes, such as the study of Buddhism.
- **Productivity/**: Reserved folder for productivity-related templates and strategy notes (currently managed alongside Self Mastery).

Notes are titled using descriptive, problem-oriented naming conventions (e.g., *2026 UAE OPEC Exit Impact Analysis*) to facilitate internal connectivity and map strategic shifts across disparate domains.

- **2026 Global Economic & Political Shifts**: Middle East volatility (UAE OPEC Exit, Strait of Hormuz), IMEC progress, Iran-US conflict, and global oil supply chain realignment.

## User Preferences

Mayank prefers a highly analytical, data-driven, and structured approach. Notes are expected to frame specific strategic shifts rather than surface-level facts, prioritizing cause-and-effect relationships.

Responses should be professional and formal for geopolitical topics, while remaining actionable and punchy for sales and self-mastery. The user values depth, nuance, and the ability to connect disparate defense challenges to broader strategic outcomes.
