# AGENTS.md

This file provides context about this Obsidian vault for AI agents.

## Vault Overview

This vault functions as a comprehensive knowledge base and strategic research repository, primarily focused on **Indian geopolitics, defense strategy, and international relations**. It synthesizes deep-dive analysis on regional security, military procurement, and global supply chains with professional development resources in sales psychology, self-mastery, and philosophical studies.

It serves as a central hub for synthesizing complex geopolitical data with actionable personal and professional development frameworks.

## Organization

The vault is organized into five thematic folders:

- **GeoPolitics & Def/**: The primary area for strategic analyses, research, and policy papers regarding Indian defense, naval presence, and regional security.
- **Sales Resources/**: Focuses on the intersection of psychology, commerce, and sales enablement checklists.
- **Self Mastery/**: Dedicated to personal growth, daily productivity guides, and mindset optimization.
- **Psychology and Religion/**: Contains historical and philosophical notes, such as the study of Buddhism.
- **Productivity/**: Reserved folder for productivity-related templates and strategy notes (currently managed alongside Self Mastery).

Additionally, the **gemini-scribe/** configuration folder contains metadata for AI agents:
- **Skills/**: Holds custom system skills with `SKILL.md` instruction guides:
  - `Geopolitical-Research/`: Geopolitical research, analysis, and scenario mapping templates.
  - `Session-Continuation/`: Protocols for resuming active research sessions (`[Scribe: Resume Session]`).
- **Prompts/**: Holds custom instruction templates, including:
  - `example-expert.md`: General subject matter expert prompt template.
  - `continuation.md`: The trigger guide for session continuation (`[Scribe: Resume Session]`).
  - `study-research-prompts.md`: Categorized prompt templates for study and research analysis.
- **Agent-Sessions/**: Active and archived logs of agent interactions.
- **example-prompts.json**: Quick-launch prompts loaded in the user interface.

Notes use a mix of kebab-case and natural language titles, often framing specific strategic pivots or problems (e.g., `The Tripwire Strategy - US Presence and Pakistan Blackmail`).

## Key Topics & Active Research

### 1. US-India-Pakistan Trilateral Dynamics & Escalations
- **The 2026 Indo-Pak Escalation**: Analysis of the sudden US diplomatic pivot from VP J.D. Vance's "none of our business" stance to ceasefire brokerage.
- **Operation Sindoor**: Indian precision strikes on Nur Khan (Rawalpindi), Sargodha (Mushaf Airbase), and Jacobabad (Shahbaz Airbase) to neutralize Pakistan's nuclear capabilities.
- **The "Tripwire" Strategy**: How Pakistan co-locates strategic assets (F-16s, tactical nuclear delivery systems) with US Technical Security Teams (TSTs) to leverage them as a human shield and force American intervention.

### 2. Indian Naval Strategy & Indo-Pacific Presence
- **Maritime Doctrines**: Transition from SAGAR to **MAHASAGAR (2025)** (Mutual and Holistic Advancement for Security and Growth Across Regions).
- **Strategic Anchors**: Fortification of **INS Jatayu** (Minicoy, Lakshadweep) monitoring the Eight Degree Channel, operationalizing the **Agalega Island** airstrip (Mauritius) for P-8I surveillance, and securing port logistics in Duqm (Oman) and Sabang (Indonesia).
- **Pax Silica & Subsea Security**: Active monitoring of subsea cables and critical minerals shipping routes.

### 3. Defense Procurement & Bureaucracy
- **Shortcomings & Systemic Issues**: Evaluation of the Defence Acquisition Procedure (DAP) 2020 and draft DAP 2026. Issues include the 12-stage cycle, GSQR "gold-plating", L1 bidder quality traps, and bureaucratic "Zero Error Syndrome".
- **Specific Bottlenecks**: HAL Tejas engine delays (GE F404 supply chain), Project 75I conventional submarine delays, and MRFA fighter jet squadron shortages.

### 4. Global Oil Supply Chain Shifts & Economic Polycrisis
- **Market Bifurcation**: Realignment of Russian crude seaborne exports to India/China utilizing a "shadow fleet" to bypass G7 price caps.
- **2026 Middle East Shock**: Impact of the Strait of Hormuz closure and the UAE exiting OPEC.
- **Indian Middle Class Squeeze**: Imported inflation (food, fuel, tech), stagnant wage growth, real-wage stagnation, and IT hiring freezes amid AI disruption.

### 5. Sales Psychology & Triggers
- **Core Principles**: Practical application of Reciprocity, Commitment & Consistency, Social Proof, Authority, Liking, and Scarcity.
- **Conversational Design**: Frame discussions as a two-way fit, ask reflective/diagnostic questions instead of checklist interrogation, and simplify offers to build trust.

### 6. Self-Mastery & Mindset Optimization
- **High-Frequency Living**: Focus on non-attachment, rebuilding self-trust with small daily promises, extreme ownership of response, and the abundance loop.
- **Productivity Systems**: Workflow optimization, rapid decision velocity, standard setting, and sustainable consistency.

### 7. Philosophy & Religion
- **Buddhism**: Objective, academic notes detailing branches, history, and key figures, contrasting with high-frequency living mindset.

## User Preferences

Mayank prefers a highly analytical, data-driven, and structured approach to research. Notes are expected to frame specific strategic shifts rather than surface-level facts, prioritizing cause-and-effect relationships.

Responses should be professional, formal for geopolitical topics, and actionable for sales/self-mastery. The user values depth, nuance, and the ability to connect disparate defense challenges to broader strategic outcomes.

- **Sales and Self Mastery**: Extract actionable psychological insights and practical applications.
- **Linking**: Use [[WikiLinks]] to connect specific defense procurement challenges to broader strategic goals (e.g., connecting a procurement failure to a specific regional security risk).
- **Nuance**: Pay special attention to themes of 'nuclear blackmail' and trilateral US-India-Pakistan relations, as these are critical pivot points in current research.
