This guide serves as a reference of prompt patterns optimized for analyzing geopolitics, defense, sales psychology, and self-mastery within this vault.

## 1. Summarization & Synthesis Prompts
*   **Purpose**: Distill lengthy articles, transcripts, or policy papers into concise notes.
*   **Prompt Template**:
    ```text
    Analyze the attached source document/video transcript and draft a minimal, structured summary. Highlight key themes, timeline of events, and direct consequences. Save the result under [Target Folder]/[Note Name].md using formal analytical language.
    ```

## 2. Deep-Dive & Cause-and-Effect Analysis Prompts
*   **Purpose**: Explore complex relationships, underlying motives, and structural dynamics.
*   **Prompt Template**:
    ```text
    Examine the relationship between [Event A] and [Event B]. Trace the specific cause-and-effect chain, identifying the underlying strategic interests of each actor involved. Present the analysis with numbered points and support it with credible references from the vault.
    ```

## 3. Strategic Cross-Linking Prompts (WikiLinking)
*   **Purpose**: Connect tactical issues to broad national security goals, keeping the knowledge base integrated.
*   **Prompt Template**:
    ```text
    Review the note on [Tactical/Procurement Bottleneck] and identify how this gap affects [Broader Strategic Goal]. Update both notes to establish bilateral connections using [[WikiLinks]] so that the relationship is clear.
    ```

## 4. Actionable Checklist & Framework Extraction Prompts
*   **Purpose**: Transform theoretical, psychological, or philosophical studies into practical daily applications.
*   **Prompt Template**:
    ```text
    Read the notes on [Core Study/Theory] and extract the key actionable takeaways. Format these into a step-by-step checklist or guide that can be easily reviewed weekly. Include a self-audit section at the end.
    ```

## 5. Scenario Mapping ("What-If" Planning) Prompts
*   **Purpose**: Project future strategic scenarios and prepare responses.
*   **Prompt Template**:
    ```text
    Draft a scenario brief outlining the impact of [Hypothetical Event, e.g., UAE exiting OPEC] on [Subject, e.g., Indian Middle Class imported inflation]. Map out a Best-Case, Worst-Case, and Most-Likely scenario, indicating specific indicators to watch.
    ```
